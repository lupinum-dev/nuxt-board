Choose the abstraction closest to the product you are building.

## Graph components

Vue Flow and similar libraries center nodes, handles, and edges. They are a strong starting point for flowcharts and graph editors. Choose Vue Board when the persistent board document and command lifecycle matter more than graph-first conventions.

## Canvas drawing primitives

Konva and Fabric expose shapes, transforms, and low-level canvas behavior. Choose them when freeform drawing, vector-like manipulation, or custom scene-graph rendering is the core requirement. Expect to design document semantics, selection policy, persistence, and history yourself.

## Complete whiteboard products

Full whiteboard SDKs provide broad toolsets and collaboration features. They reduce initial product work but bring a larger prescribed model. Choose them when the finished whiteboard experience matters more than owning a narrow Vue-specific engine.

## Static diagram renderers

Mermaid and similar tools turn descriptions into diagrams. Use them when users do not need direct spatial editing.

Vue Board occupies the middle: more product semantics than drawing primitives, less product prescription than a complete whiteboard, and a document-first model rather than a graph-first component.
